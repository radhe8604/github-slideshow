STREAM PROTOCOL SUPPORT
========================

Protocol    Extension    Support Level    Notes
────────    ─────────    ─────────────    ─────
HLS         .m3u8        ✅ Full          Best compatibility
DASH        .mpd         ✅ Full          Modern browsers
RTMP        rtmp://      ⚠️  Limited      Needs conversion
SRT         srt://       ⚠️  Limited      Needs conversion

TROUBLESHOOTING
===============

Issue: Stream won't play
Fix: Check browser console, verify stream URL

Issue: Page not loading
Fix: Run jekyll build, check for errors

Issue: Styles not applying
Fix: Hard refresh browser (Ctrl+F5)

Issue: HLS not working
Fix: Check HLS.js is loaded, try Safari
